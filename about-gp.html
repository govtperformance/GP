About Government Performance

A civic enablement platform built to help communities raise responsible awareness.

Government Performance (GP) was created to help citizens and citizen journalists bring responsible visibility to local government decisions, actions, and policies that affect their communities.

In many small cities and rural counties, citizens often lack the tools, knowledge, or confidence to pursue issues that affect them. GP exists to close that gap by providing structure, guidance, and practical capabilities that help individuals act responsibly and effectively.

Founder Leadership

Founder-driven, mission-focused, and community practical.

Government Performance is shaped by founder leadership focused on helping citizens and citizen journalists bring responsible visibility to local government issues that have real community impact.

Operational Support and Fundraising

Jeff also helps coordinate fundraising support from successful small-town entrepreneurs who understand the challenges local citizens face when trying to raise awareness around important government issues. That support helps sustain GP operations and expand the tools, solutions, and capabilities the platform can provide to citizens and citizen journalists working on validated, high-impact concerns.

Government Performance operates through a unique operational model designed to identify and focus attention on real issues affecting small communities. The model is structured to move beyond noise, speculation, and online chatter, allowing GP to concentrate on concerns that have genuine community impact and warrant responsible public visibility.

GP experts work directly with citizens and citizen journalists to understand reported concerns, assess their relevance to the community, and guide the fact-finding process needed to validate those issues. This includes organizing information, obtaining public records, and assembling credible documentation that supports responsible investigative review.

When concerns are validated, Government Performance helps develop investigative reports and supporting materials that can be responsibly positioned across local and regional media markets, community social media environments, civic networks, and relevant online influencers to expand community awareness.

This operational model enables Government Performance to maximize visibility around meaningful civic issues while maintaining discipline around accuracy, validation, and community relevance — advancing GP’s mission of Communities Holding Government Accountable.

Why GP Exists

Across many communities, citizens often recognize issues that affect their town, county, or region but lack practical ways to investigate them, obtain relevant information, or organize constructive awareness.

Government Performance provides the framework that helps citizens move from concern to informed civic action by offering tools, guidance, and communications structure.

What Makes GP Different

Government Performance is not a complaint forum and not a social media debate group.

GP is a civic infrastructure platform designed to help citizens responsibly research issues, obtain documentation, organize information, and communicate clearly with their communities.

Founding Perspective

Executive business leaders giving back and helping citizens access civic capabilities.

Government Performance was founded and supported by experienced business leaders who understand the challenges citizens face when attempting to raise awareness around local government issues.

Many citizens recognize issues affecting their communities but lack the tools and guidance needed to pursue them responsibly. GP provides the digital infrastructure, templates, and communications guidance that enable individuals to act effectively while maintaining responsible civic engagement.

Communities holding government accountable for the issues they want addressed.
