Our Mission

Communities Holding Government Accountable.

Government Performance helps small and rural communities move from concern to documented understanding, from verified findings to public awareness, and from awareness toward informed civic action.

Our Operating Model

Professional expertise. Citizen engagement. Targeted action.

GP experts work alongside citizens and volunteers to gather facts, assess impacts, and prepare investigative findings. Where warranted, GP positions reports and supporting information with local and regional news outlets, community networks, social media groups, and relevant online voices to broaden responsible public awareness.

The goal is not simply to generate attention. It is to equip communities with verified information that supports informed discussion, meaningful scrutiny, and accountable government decision-making.

Why GP Exists

In small cities and rural counties, residents may see consequential government decisions unfolding without the investigative resources, access to information, or media attention needed to examine them thoroughly.

GP helps bridge that gap by bringing structure, expertise, and coordinated support to the work of understanding and documenting important local issues.

What Makes GP Different

GP is not a complaint forum, political advocacy group, or social media debate platform. We focus on evidence, community relevance, and responsible public communication.

Rather than amplifying every concern, GP evaluates issues, validates facts, and concentrates its efforts where credible investigative work and public awareness can make a difference.

Leadership and Community Support

Led by people who care about the future of small and rural communities.

GP brings together experienced business leaders, community leaders, subject-matter experts, and committed volunteers who recognize the challenges facing smaller communities. Their collective expertise, relationships, and practical experience help guide investigations, support responsible reporting, and connect important findings with the audiences who need to hear them.

GP also engages community-minded entrepreneurs and other supporters who share a commitment to informed civic participation and effective local government. This leadership-and-volunteer model helps direct scarce resources toward issues with the greatest potential community impact.

What GP Does

Turning community concerns into credible findings and meaningful public awareness.

GP works directly with residents and citizen journalists to identify concerns, evaluate their community impact, and determine which issues warrant deeper attention. We move beyond speculation and online chatter to focus limited resources on substantiated matters that can significantly affect a community.

Listen and assess

Engage citizens, understand reported concerns, and assess their relevance, seriousness, and potential community impact.

Research and validate

Coordinate fact-finding, public records requests, document review, and on-the-ground research to establish what the evidence supports.

Investigate and inform

Develop credible investigative reports and communicate validated findings through appropriate local, regional, and digital media channels.
