GP Impact

How citizens have used GP structure, guidance, and tools to raise awareness and encourage local government action.

Across the country, citizens and citizen journalists are using Government Performance’s guidance, tools, communications structure, research support, and issue organization capabilities to responsibly raise awareness around local government decisions and actions affecting their communities. Government Performance does not take over local issues. Instead, GP helps citizens define the issue, obtain relevant information, organize communication, and engage their communities effectively. When concerns are validated and linked to proven community impact, GP can also support deeper investigative reporting and responsible media publishing.

Community Experiences and Outcomes

Citizens using structure, guidance, and tools to raise awareness.

These examples reflect the kind of practical enablement GP is built to provide. Citizens drove the issue. GP helped them move forward with more structure, clarity, and confidence.

Southeast Gulf Coast Community — Coastal Zoning Transparency

“Our town was about to approve a zoning change that would have dramatically increased density along our waterfront. Most residents didn’t even know it was happening until it was nearly finalized.

Government Performance helped me understand how to obtain the planning documents, structure a public records request, and organize the issue so people could clearly understand the potential impact. They also helped me create a simple summary that could be shared within the community.

Once the information was organized and shared, awareness grew quickly. Attendance at the next planning meeting increased significantly and the city agreed to delay the vote so the proposal could receive additional public review.

GP didn’t run the issue for me — they showed me how to do it correctly and responsibly. That structure made all the difference.”

Outcome: Planning documents were obtained through public records, the issue summary was shared across the community, public meeting attendance increased significantly, and the zoning proposal was returned for additional review.

— Michael R.

Western Rural County Community — Infrastructure Contract Review

“In our county, most people assumed contracts were decided long before citizens ever heard about them. I noticed a road improvement contract that didn’t seem to follow the normal bidding timeline, but I had no idea how to investigate it.

Government Performance showed me how to organize the information I had, identify what records to request, and present the issue in a way that stayed factual and constructive.

Once the documentation was obtained and shared within the community, county officials reviewed the procurement process and clarified the bidding timeline before final approval.

GP provided the structure and guidance that allowed me to pursue the issue responsibly and effectively.”

Outcome: Procurement documentation was obtained, community awareness increased, county officials reviewed the contract process, and transparency improved in infrastructure bidding procedures.

— Daniel H.

Central Regional Community — School District Budget Transparency

“Parents in our district were concerned about a proposed administrative expansion that would increase costs, but the information available publicly was extremely limited.

Government Performance helped me understand how to obtain the relevant budget materials and how to organize the information so other parents could understand the financial impact.

Once the information was clearly presented, more parents became engaged in the discussion and the school board scheduled an additional public session to review the proposal.

GP didn’t push the issue — they helped me bring it forward in a responsible and organized way.”

Outcome: Budget documentation was obtained, the parent community became more informed and engaged, the school board scheduled an additional public review session, and transparency improved around district financial decisions.

— Laura M.

North County Community — Land Development and Environmental Review

“A large land clearing project began near our community and residents were concerned about drainage and environmental impact, but nobody seemed to know who approved it or what permits existed.

Government Performance helped me determine which agencies held the relevant permits and how to request the information properly.

After the documentation was obtained and shared within the community, county officials conducted an additional review of the permitting process.

GP helped me move from concern to informed action in a responsible way.”

Outcome: Permit documentation was obtained from county and state agencies, community awareness increased, county officials conducted an additional review, and environmental considerations were evaluated before project continuation.

— Thomas W.
