What it is and its mission

A serious national platform built around citizen enablement.

Government Performance is a national civic infrastructure platform that helps citizens and citizen journalists understand how to act when local government decisions, policies, or actions affect their communities. GP provides structure, guidance, and communications capabilities that enable people to move from concern to informed action.

GP exists because many communities do not lack concern — they lack safe, practical, credible ways to investigate issues, communicate clearly, and build community understanding around them. GP closes that gap through tools, templates, how-tos, communications guidance, and awareness-building capabilities that help citizens act on their own without unnecessary risk.

The challenge

Why local citizens often need more support than they can find on their own.

Across many smaller communities, local issues can be real, consequential, and difficult to bring forward responsibly without better tools, structure, and visibility.

Fear of retaliation

Citizens may fear social, professional, or political consequences for speaking up in smaller communities.

Weak local media

Coverage can be limited or nonexistent, leaving important issues without public attention.

Low public awareness

Government decisions can move forward without broad community understanding or engagement.

Tight information networks

Informal networks may shape what is heard, what is ignored, and who is given access or opportunity.

Research and records gaps

Citizens may not know how to focus research, request records, or obtain information that validates the issue.

Communication limitations

Communities may lack the know-how to use social platforms, surveys, video, and direct messaging effectively.

How GP works

A guided process that keeps the citizen in control.

GP does not take over local issues. It helps citizens and citizen journalists define them more clearly, choose practical next steps, and use the right communications and research tools to build awareness responsibly.

01

Citizen submits a concern

Baseline information, location, context, and links or materials help establish that the issue is real and community-relevant.

02

GP reviews and clarifies

GP may request additional details to better define the issue and identify practical next steps.

03

A baseline plan is outlined

Depending on the situation, GP may recommend research, records requests, communications steps, awareness actions, or engagement tactics.

04

Tools and guidance are provided

Citizens receive templates, how-tos, messaging guidance, and practical support so they can act effectively and responsibly.

05

The citizen drives the issue forward

GP is an enablement platform, not the responsible driver of the issue. The citizen remains the active force behind awareness and action.

What our capabilities enable

Practical tools and guidance that help citizens move with confidence.

GP provides baseline technology services, issue support, communications guidance, research support, messaging structure, and awareness-building capabilities that help citizens act effectively without needing specialized infrastructure of their own. For validated issues with high community impact, GP can also support investigative reporting and responsible media publishing designed to expand visibility and accountability.

Issue research guidance

Direction on how to focus research, identify the right facts, and pursue information relevant to the issue at hand.

Public records support

Templates, structure, and how-to guidance for records requests and evidence gathering that supports responsible awareness building.

Citizen journalism enablement

Tools and practical guidance that help citizens document, frame, and communicate local issues with clarity and credibility.

Awareness campaign tools

Support for building issue visibility through web, social platforms, surveys, direct messaging, and community engagement tactics.

Video and content direction

Guidance on using video, written reports, and issue summaries to help communities understand what is happening and why it matters.

Privacy-oriented communications

Approaches and front-end communication options that help reduce personal risk while enabling responsible public engagement.

Operating principle

GP is an enablement platform — not the responsible driver of the issue.

GP supports citizens who are willing to invest time in real issues that affect their communities. It is not designed for generalized complaining, disruption, or unsupported accusations. The citizen or citizen journalist remains the person advancing the issue behind the scenes, while GP helps with tools, structure, and strategic guidance.
