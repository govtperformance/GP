Mission

Equip communities with the tools they need to hold government accountable for the issues and actions they want addressed.

GP exists to help citizens and citizen journalists move from concern to credible civic action. It does this by offering practical guidance, communications support, research direction, templates, how-tos, awareness-building tools, and issue-development structure. For validated concerns linked to proven community impact, GP may also support more formal investigative reporting and media publishing.

Operating model

GP is the infrastructure. The citizen remains the actor.

GP does not replace the citizen, speak over the community, or position itself as the primary owner of every issue. It provides a disciplined structure that helps committed citizens responsibly investigate, communicate, and build public awareness around matters affecting their communities.

What GP is not

Designed for real issues, not generalized noise.

Clear boundaries strengthen trust, improve credibility, and protect the seriousness of the platform.

Not a complaint forum

GP is not a place for generalized venting or unsupported attacks.

Not a whistleblower dump site

Submissions are expected to include validating context, relevance, and a real connection to the issue.

Not the issue owner

GP provides enablement and structure. The citizen remains the responsible local driver.
