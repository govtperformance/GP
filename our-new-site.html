Our New Site!

A more streamlined home for Government Performance materials, tools, and reports.

This is our new streamlined site and it will serve as the primary location for Government Performance materials, tools, and reports. We are taking a technology-minimalistic approach to streamline information and resource flow because our mission is small-community government transparency — not cutting-edge technology for its own sake.

Why we changed

A simpler site makes it easier to focus on the work.

Government Performance supports citizens and citizen journalists with practical tools, research guidance, issue organization, and reporting support. This site is designed to reduce complexity and make it easier to move information, documents, and community concerns through the platform.

Built for clarity

Free-form concern submission is now easier.

We have added a new concern submission approach that better enables free-form information to be provided to Government Performance. Citizens can now use a guided submission screen online or download a PDF form, complete it, and submit it by email.

New concern process

How to download and submit the concern form

Use whichever path works best for you. The goal is to make it easier to provide complete information, context, and supporting materials.

Option 1: Use the submission screen

Open the new Submit a Concern page and use the structured guidance to prepare your information.

Option 2: Download the PDF form

Download the PDF submission form, fill it in electronically or by hand, and save or scan it when complete.

Send by email

Email the completed form and any supporting documents to concerns@GovtPerformance.com.

Feedback

With all new things comes the potential for new issues.

If you use the site and encounter issues, please let us know. Your feedback helps us improve the flow of information and resources across the platform.
