Submit a Concern

Tell us what is happening in your community.

Share a local government concern with Government Performance. Describe the issue in your own words and provide the facts, links, and context available to you.

How to use this process

Two easy ways to submit your concern

Choose the option that works best for you. Both methods send your concern to Government Performance by email.

Option 1

Complete and Email the Online Form

Enter your information in the form on this page, then select Continue to Email & Send. Your email application opens with your responses ready to review. Click Send in your email application to complete the submission.

Option 2

Download and Complete the PDF

Download the PDF form, complete it at your convenience, and email the form and any supporting documents to concerns@GovtPerformance.com.

Email submission address

concerns@GovtPerformance.com

Describe the issue in free form, not just short fields.
Include links, documents, photos, agendas, meeting materials, or records if available.
Explain how the issue impacts the community or a part of the community.
State what help or guidance you are seeking from Government Performance.
