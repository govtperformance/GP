Submit a Concern

A new concern submission screen designed for clearer, freer-form reporting to GP.

Use this streamlined process to provide information, links, documents, and context about a local government issue affecting your community. The process is designed to help citizens and citizen journalists provide more complete information without forcing the issue into a rigid format.

How to use this process

Share the concern in your own words, with the details that matter.

The best submissions explain what is happening, who is involved, why it matters, and what materials support the concern. You can either use the guided structure below or download the PDF form and email it to Government Performance.

Email submission address

concerns@GovtPerformance.com

Describe the issue in free form, not just short fields.
Include links, documents, photos, agendas, meeting materials, or records if available.
Explain how the issue impacts the community or a part of the community.
State what help or guidance you are seeking from Government Performance.
